ICE Performance Recorder installation
=====================================

Copy ice-rlcraft-profiler-<version>.jar into mods.
This JAR contains recording, reports and the optional deep bytecode probes.
Stop Minecraft/server before upgrading. Remove older profiler Main/Core JARs;
keep the optimizer JAR and other mods. Configuration, reports and saves can stay.
The profiler remains optional and is distributed separately from the optimizer.

The workspace tools/deploy-profiler.ps1 accepts -Archive and -ModsDirectory,
backs up only matching profiler JARs, and prints a RollbackManifest path.
Restore that installation with -RollbackManifest <path> after stopping Minecraft.

安装方法：把 ice-rlcraft-profiler-<版本>.jar 放入 mods。
这个 JAR 已包含记录、报告以及可选的深度字节码探针。
升级前关闭游戏/服务端，移除旧版 profiler 主/Core JAR，保留 optimizer
和其他模组。配置、已有报告和存档可以保留。Profiler 仍单独发布、按需安装。

工作区 tools/deploy-profiler.ps1 支持 -Archive 和 -ModsDirectory，
只备份与迁移 Profiler JAR，并输出 RollbackManifest 路径。
关闭游戏后用 -RollbackManifest <路径> 可恢复该次安装前的 Profiler 文件。
