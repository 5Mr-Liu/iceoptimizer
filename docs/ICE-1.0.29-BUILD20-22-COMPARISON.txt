NEW ALGORITHM: stop sorting queries after the last permitted occluder write.
The original path kept extracting a near-first heap after its128-face render
budget was exhausted. Build22 scans the remaining heap entries linearly.
It uses the unchanged conservative depth proof, keeps partially evaluated and
untouched entries visible when the cell budget runs out, and preserves final
draw order. It does not reuse visibility across frames or change raster math.
Tail work drops from O(n log n) extraction to O(n) queries; storage is unchanged.
At tight budgets the chosen hidden subset may differ, but every hidden entry
must also be hidden by an unlimited-budget reference raster.
